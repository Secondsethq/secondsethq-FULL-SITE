Questions about marketing and web design services.

Marketing, web design, local SEO, CRM and bookkeeping in Vista, CA.

Which service should I start with?

Whichever thing has been stuck the longest. Most owners in Vista and North County San Diego start with the website or the follow-up, because those two cost you money every week they are broken. We will tell you honestly if something else should come first.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Every service on this page can be bought on its own. We are two specialists rather than an agency with a bundle to sell, so you take what you need and leave the rest.

Do you work with businesses outside Vista?

Yes. We work across North County San Diego and the Northern Colorado Front Range in person, and remotely anywhere. Bookkeeping and CRM work in particular do not care where you are.

How do you price marketing and operations work?

Either a fixed package price agreed before we start, or an hourly rate for smaller pieces. Whichever it is, you get the number in writing first and it does not move unless you change the scope.

What if I need two services at once?

That is common, and it is why there are two of us. One handles brand, websites, copy and campaigns. One handles CRM, automation, systems and books. They run in parallel rather than queueing behind each other.

How long does a typical project take?

A single service like local SEO or a CRM cleanup is usually two to four weeks. A five-page website build is three to four weeks, and brand and website together is five to seven. Bookkeeping catch-up depends entirely on how far behind the books are.
