Why Johnstown is different

Northern Colorado is not the Denver metro.

Johnstown was one of the fastest-growing communities in the country in the year to July 2025, among towns of twenty thousand or more. The town sits where I-25 meets Highway 34, alongside the 2534 development, Ledge Rock Center and a Class-A industrial park, and it issued 18 commercial permits in 2025 after 21 the year before. Every one of those is work for somebody's crew.

Businesses that write themselves as Denver-area lose twice. They are invisible for the searches that would actually reach them, and buried for the ones they claimed. Johnstown, Fort Collins, Greeley and Windsor are what people type, and those are the words worth owning.

The AI question

What AI search changes, and what it doesn't.

You have probably been told you need to rewrite everything for AI. For a local business that is close to the least effective thing you could spend money on, and we would rather explain why than sell it to you.

What is genuinely worth doing

  • Submit your sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools, since ChatGPT search runs on that index
  • FAQ content phrased the way people actually ask, with short extractable answers
  • Get mentioned on third-party pages: chamber, local press, supplier listings
  • Fix name, address, and phone consistency, which is AI optimization by another name
  • Run ten real customer questions through the AI engines and note who gets named

What is mostly being oversold

  • Rewriting your website copy to sound appealing to a language model
  • Paying for tools that promise AI ranking, which is not a thing you can buy
  • Publishing volumes of blog posts a local service business does not need
  • Chasing domain authority, which is a third-party invention and not a Google metric
  • Treating AI as a replacement channel when traditional organic still sends far more

Fixing your listings is AI search optimization, whatever anybody chooses to call it.

Proof

Where this framework came from.

Alchemy Print Co. is a screen printing shop in Vista. Auditing them is what produced our Local SEO Standard, which we now run on every client before quoting any work.

What the audit found
  • Scored 4 out of 25 on the three signals that carry the most ranking weight: title, H1, and meta description.
  • The homepage title contained neither "screen printing" nor "Vista." The site did not appear in the first four pages for its own main search term.
  • Listings disagreed with each other on the business name, the phone number, and the founding year.
  • Every call to action on one landing page linked to nothing, and the phone link would not dial.
What we did
  • Rewrote titles, headings, and copy to name the service and each city in the service area.

  • Added LocalBusiness with an 18-city areaServed, plus Service and FAQ markup matching every visible question.
  • Put a working, tracked quote form on every page.

Being straight about the limits: the rebuilt pages are live and indexed, but ranking outcomes take months to settle, so we are not claiming positions we have not earned yet. What we will claim is that the business is now findable, consistent, and machine-readable, which it demonstrably was not before.

Questions

The things people actually ask.

What is local SEO?
Local SEO is the work that makes a business show up when someone nearby searches for what it sells. Most of it happens off your website: your Google Business Profile, your listings, and whether your name, address, and phone match everywhere. The site matters, but less than people assume.
How long does local SEO take to work?
Listing and profile fixes can show up in weeks. Ranking movement in the map pack usually takes two to four months, and competitive terms take longer. Anyone promising page one in thirty days is either guessing or describing a term nobody searches for.
Why does my business name have to match everywhere?
Google cross-references your name, address, and phone across the web to decide how confident it is that you are real and where you are. One listing saying Alchemy Print and another saying Alchemy Print Co. reads as two businesses, and that uncertainty costs you visibility.
Will you help me get more Google reviews?
We will set up the ask properly and make it easy for happy customers. What we will not do is gate reviews, offer incentives, or write them, all of which Google explicitly banned in its 2026 policy updates and now actively enforces. Yelp also prohibits asking outright.
Does AI search change any of this?
Less than you have been told. Roughly 85 percent of brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party pages rather than the brand's own website. So fixing your listings and getting mentioned elsewhere is AI search optimization, whatever anybody chooses to call it.
Should I rewrite my website for ChatGPT?
For a local business that is close to the least effective lever available. Traditional organic still sends vastly more traffic than the AI engines combined, and the citations mostly come from third-party pages. Fix the local proof first and the AI visibility follows.
What is a NAP audit?
NAP stands for name, address, phone. We search your business, open every listing on page one, and write down exactly what each one says. Any difference is a finding. It sounds trivial and it is routinely the highest-value hour of work on a local account.
Do I need to be on every directory?
No. A handful of platforms carry almost all the weight: Google Business Profile, Yelp, your Chamber of Commerce, and in suburban neighborhoods Nextdoor, where recommendation threads drive real local work. Beyond those, more listings mostly means more places for your details to quietly drift out of sync.
What does local SEO cost?
A one-time audit and cleanup across your website and every listing is $1,400. Ongoing work is quoted by scope, and smaller pieces are $85 an hour. We tell you on the first call which of the three you actually need, including when the answer is none of them.
How do I know if it is working?
By calls, direction requests, and form submissions, not by rankings. We set up tracking so you can see inquiries by source, and every quarter we run ten real customer questions through Google, ChatGPT, and Perplexity and write down which businesses get named.
