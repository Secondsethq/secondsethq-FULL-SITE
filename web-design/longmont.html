Why Longmont is different

An audience with very fast internet.

Longmont has municipal fibre, and a population that came here partly because of it. Manufacturing, tech, and a downtown rebuilt around businesses that expect things to work. Your visitors have some of the fastest home connections in the country.

That cuts both ways. A heavy site will load fine for them and terribly for anyone on a phone outside town, and you will never hear about it. It also means the audience has a high floor for what a working website looks like, and notices immediately when something is held together with plugins.

The AI question

What AI builders do, and what they don't.

AI website builders are genuinely good now, and we would rather tell you where the line is than pretend they do not exist. Plenty of businesses should start with one.

What they do well

  • Get something presentable online in an afternoon
  • Produce a clean, modern-looking layout
  • Draft passable first-pass copy
  • Cost far less than hiring anybody
  • Let you change things yourself without asking permission

Where they leave you short

  • Generic copy that describes what you do, not what the customer gets
  • Titles and headings that never name your actual service or your city
  • No structured data, which is how machines understand a local business
  • Forms that submit into nowhere, or into an inbox nobody checks
  • No idea whether any of it converted, because nothing is tracked

A website that looks good and cannot be found is a business card you paid to host.

The gap between a template and something that brings work in

If you have already built something with an AI tool, bring it. Often the structure is fine and the fix is the copy, the tags, the forms, and the tracking. That is cheaper than starting over and we will say so.

Questions

The things people actually ask.

How much does a small business website cost?
Our website package is $3,400 for up to five pages with copywriting included, two rounds of revisions, and full handover. Brand and website together is $6,400. Smaller pieces are $85 an hour. A single landing page is two hours for the first and one hour for each page after.
How long does it take to build a website?
Three to four weeks for a five-page build, and five to seven weeks if we are building the brand alongside it. Week one is structure and copy, weeks two and three are design and build, and the last stretch is revisions, testing, and handover.
What is the difference between a website and a landing page?
A full website tells people who you are and covers everything you do. A landing page has one job and one button, usually tied to a single service or campaign. Landing pages convert better because they remove every choice except the one you want.
Can I edit my website myself after it launches?
Yes, and you should be able to. Handover includes editing access and a walkthrough recording so you can change text, swap photos, and update hours without calling us. You own the files, the logins, and the domain. Nothing is held hostage.
Can I just use an AI website builder instead?
For a first page, often yes. AI builders produce something that looks fine quickly. What they do not produce is a website that names your service and your city where it counts, loads fast, has working forms, and carries structured data. Those are the parts that get you found.
Who writes the copy for my website?
We do, and it is included in the package price. If you would rather write it yourself we take $600 off. Most owners think they want to write it and then find the blank page is exactly why it has been stuck for eight months.
Will my website show up on Google?
It will be built to. Titles, headings, and copy that name the service and the city, structured data, fast load times, and a working form. Ranking itself takes months and depends on your local competition, so we do not promise positions, only the work that earns them.
Do I need a new website or just a better one?
Often just a better one. If the structure is sound and only the copy, speed, and forms are broken, a rebuild wastes money. We tell you which one you are looking at on the first call, even when the answer is the cheaper one for you.
What if I already have a domain and hosting?
We build around what you already pay for. Keeping your domain, your host, and your email means less disruption and no new subscriptions. If a platform genuinely cannot do the job we will say so before you spend anything on moving.
How many revisions are included?
Two rounds on every package. A round means you gather all your notes and send them at once, then we work through them together. That is enough for almost every project. A third round is billed at the hourly rate of $85.
Do you work with Longmont businesses in person?
Yes. We are a marketing and systems studio based in Vista, California, with a Northern Colorado operation, so we work with businesses across Northern Colorado and the Front Range regularly. Almost all of the build is remote, and we are glad to meet up when it helps.
