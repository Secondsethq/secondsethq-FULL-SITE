The AI question

AI is only as good as the data underneath it.

Every CRM now sells an AI layer, and some of it genuinely helps. None of it helps if the records it reads are duplicated, half-filled, and two years stale.

What AI genuinely does well

  • Summarizes a long thread into what actually matters
  • Drafts replies and meeting notes in seconds
  • Scores leads once there is real history to score against
  • Flags deals that have gone quiet
  • Answers questions about your data in plain language

What it cannot do on its own

  • Decide what a pipeline stage means for your business
  • Keep records clean as contacts move, merge, and change
  • Notice that a workflow silently stopped firing five weeks ago
  • Reconcile three tools that disagree about the same customer
  • Make anyone want to use a system that is more work than not using it

Automate a broken process and you get a broken process, faster.

Questions

The things people actually ask.

What does a CRM actually do?
It keeps every customer, inquiry, and deal in one place with a record of what happened and what is next. The value is not the software, it is that the business stops running out of your head, your inbox, and three spreadsheets that disagree.
Which CRM should I use?
Usually the one you already pay for. HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Ontraport, and Pipedrive all work for a small business. Switching is expensive and rarely the real problem. A half-finished setup is the problem, and that is fixable in whatever you own.
My CRM is a mess. Is it worth fixing or should I start over?
Almost always worth fixing. Duplicate contacts, deals stuck in the wrong stage, and fields nobody fills in are all repairable. Starting over means re-importing the same mess with more steps. We audit first and tell you honestly which one you are looking at.
How long does a CRM setup take?
Two to three weeks for most small businesses. Week one is mapping how work actually moves through your business, which is usually different from how anyone thinks it does. Week two is building it, and week three is testing with real deals before handover.
Can you connect my CRM to my other tools?
Yes, that is most of the job. Forms, calendars, email, invoicing, and phone all feeding one record. We build integrations in Zapier or the platform's own connectors, in an account you own, so nothing depends on us continuing to be involved.
Will my team actually use it?
Only if it is less work than not using it. That is a design problem, not a training problem. We cut the required fields to the minimum, automate anything that can be filled in automatically, and remove every step that exists only for reporting.
What is the difference between a CRM and a spreadsheet?
A spreadsheet records what happened. A CRM also tells you what is next and reminds somebody to do it. If your spreadsheet is working and nothing is slipping, keep it. Most people find things slip quietly and they only notice at the end of a slow month.
Do you provide training and documentation?
Yes, and it is included. You get a walkthrough recording, written documentation of how the pipeline works, and the reasoning behind the setup. If someone new joins in a year they can read it rather than guess or call us.
Can AI run my CRM for me?
It can help with drafting, summarizing, and scoring. It cannot decide what a stage means for your business, keep your data clean, or notice when a workflow silently stopped firing. Those are setup and maintenance jobs, and they are where CRMs actually fail.
