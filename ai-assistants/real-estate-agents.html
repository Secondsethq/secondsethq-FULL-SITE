Questions

The things agents actually ask.

What is an AI assistant for a real estate agent?
A set of small, focused helpers that each do one job: a morning brief, lead replies, follow-up drafts, listing marketing, social posts. They read your CRM, calendar and email, do the drafting and sorting, and hand you the result to approve. Think of it as a team that works your CRM every day.
Will it send things to my clients without asking me?
No. Every email, text and post waits for your OK. The assistants draft, sort and fill in templates. You decide what goes out, and nothing is sent in your name until you approve it.
Which CRMs does it work with?
Most real estate CRMs connect, either through their own app connection or through Zapier. What each one allows varies, so we check yours before we quote. If a CRM only allows part of what we need, we tell you which assistants can act and which can only draft.
Do I need to set up my CRM first?
It helps, because the assistants are only as good as what is in the CRM. If yours is half set up, we fix it first as part of the same project. If it is already in good shape, we build straight on top of it.
Who owns the account and my data?
You do. The assistants run in your own AI account and connect to your own CRM, email and calendar. We build and maintain them from there, but your client data never moves into an account of ours.
Can it pull listings from the MLS?
No. MLS data is licensed, so you export what you are allowed to use and the assistants work from that. We do not scrape the MLS, Facebook or any other site.
Does this replace my assistant or transaction coordinator?
Usually not. Most solo agents we talk to have no assistant at all, so this is new capacity rather than a replacement. If you do have one, the assistants take the typing and sorting off their plate so their hours go to the work that needs a person.
What happens when something breaks?
Monthly care covers it. Tools change their screens and connections, and an assistant can stop working when they do. We fix it, usually within one business day, and each assistant comes with a written fallback so the work still gets done that day.
What does it cost?
Setup of the five starter assistants is $1,500, once. Monthly care is $250 a month and covers fixes, updates and small changes. Add-on assistants such as listing marketing or open house kits start at $350 each. Your own AI and software subscriptions are paid directly by you.
