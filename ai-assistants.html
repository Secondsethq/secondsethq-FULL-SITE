Questions

The things people actually ask.

What is an AI assistant, in plain terms?
A small helper that does one repeatable job in your business, like drafting replies, sorting new inquiries or writing a weekly post. It reads the tools you already use and hands you the result to approve. You get a set of them, each with one job.
Is this a chatbot on my website?
No. These work behind the scenes in your CRM, email, calendar and design tools. Nothing talks to your customers on its own, and nothing is sent in your name without your approval.
Which industries do you build for?
Real estate agents first, because their work repeats in clear patterns: leads, follow-up, listings and events. We build for other owner-run businesses on request, after a scoping call to see whether the work is repeatable enough to be worth it.
Who owns the accounts and data?
You do. The assistants run in your own AI account and connect to your own tools. We build and maintain them from there, and your customer data never moves into an account of ours.
Why does my CRM matter?
Because the assistants work from what is in it. A CRM with duplicates and stale records gives them bad information. If yours needs work, we fix it first with our CRM setup, then build on top.
What does it cost?
For real estate agents, setup of the five starter assistants is $1,500, once, and monthly care is $250 a month. Add-on assistants start at $350 each. Other industries are quoted after a scoping call.
