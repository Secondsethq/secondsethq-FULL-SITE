Escrow · North County San Diego

Escrow Ali

A brand, a website, a rebuilt CRM and the automation behind it for Ali Jackson, VP of Business Development at California Coast Escrow. Built while she changed companies, and live in time for her announcement email to reach 1,803 agents with a 54.7% open rate.

The brief

Ali calls herself a connector. She runs two weekly agent caravans, a monthly pitch event called Hot Properties, and a network of more than 1,500 agents across San Diego County and the Temecula Valley. All of it lived in her head, her inbox and a CRM that had just shut down.

She was also moving to a new escrow company, with an announcement going out to her whole database in under three weeks. So the work had to land in order: get the contacts out and cleaned, rebuild the CRM, give her a brand and a site agents could land on, then make the weekly work run without her.

She isn't someone who wants to learn software. Everything was built so her assistant can run it from written steps, and so Ali only does the parts that need her.

What we delivered

  • Brand identity & logo system
  • Hot Properties event logo
  • Website design & build, 28 pages
  • Wise Agent CRM rebuild
  • Contact cleanup & import
  • Daily voicemail & event automation
  • Email library & automatic drips
  • Event sign-in signs & print
  • Canva brand kit & templates for her team

At the door

Sign-in signs at the door.

Every agent who walks into a caravan or an event is a contact Ali wants to keep. These sit on an easel at the door. The QR code opens a quick sign-in on the agent's own phone, so Ali leaves with names and numbers instead of a stack of business cards to type up. One for each kind of event, all at 6 by 8 inches.

The codes are blurred here so the signs on this page don't add test entries to her list.

CRM & automation

The weekly work, written down and running.

Her old CRM had shut down. We rebuilt everything in Wise Agent, then turned each recurring job into a checklist or an automation her assistant runs from written steps.

01

Contacts, cleaned and imported

More than 1,500 agents merged from old exports, email history and business card scans, deduplicated and tagged by role, area, tier and how well Ali knows them.

02

Daily caravan voicemails

New listing alerts arrive at 4 AM. Her assistant sorts them by city, schedules a voicemail invite to each listing agent for the right caravan, and logs it on the contact.

03

Event checklists

Templates for Hot Properties, both caravans, video days, caravan thank-yous and business card batches. Start one with a date and every task lands on the right person.

04

RSVPs straight into the CRM

Every Eventbrite RSVP is added to Wise Agent automatically, with the agent's phone and brokerage, so event guests don't need retyping.

05

Emails that send themselves

A new-agent welcome series, an after-closing series and seasonal emails to her clients, all written in her voice and switched on.

06

Three businesses, kept apart

Separate sending addresses for her escrow work, her pitch events and her real estate business, so an unsubscribe in one doesn't silence the others.

For her team

A brand her assistant can't get wrong.

Ali's assistant makes most of her graphics, so the brand had to live where that work happens. We set up her Canva brand kit with every logo, both palettes and the fonts, and organized it into folders by job. The template set fills those folders, so a new flyer is a change of date and a photo, not a design project.

  • Hot Properties event kit: Eventbrite banner, sponsor flyer and social post
  • Weekly content series covers: Market Monday, Tuesday Tips and Know Your Escrow
  • Holiday posts with matching Instagram stories, and YouTube thumbnails
  • A LinkedIn kit, email headers and flyers, all with photo slots
  • Weekly content tasks assigned to her assistant inside Wise Agent
